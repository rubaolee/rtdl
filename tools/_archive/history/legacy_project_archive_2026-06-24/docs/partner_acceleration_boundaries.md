# Partner Acceleration Boundaries

Status: suspended for V3 rebuild on 2026-06-20.

Partner guidance is not currently a public release promise. The previous page
has been reduced because it described old V3 behavior as current.

## Current Rule

Until the V3 rebuild gate passes:

- do not claim arbitrary partner acceleration;
- do not claim automatic partner selection;
- do not claim broad backend speedup;
- do not present old partner matrices as current guidance;
- do not publish performance wording without exact pod artifacts.

## What May Still Be Said

RTDL may use partner-owned arrays in development examples and benchmark
candidates. Those candidates must be classified before they become user-facing
guidance.

Every future partner claim must name:

- the primitive or benchmark row;
- the partner;
- the backend;
- the output contract;
- the hardware;
- the command line;
- the artifact path;
- the row classification.
