# Particle post-formal compiler-artifact reconstruction

Date: 2026-09-09

## Why this exists

Lead review found that the original `110dee7aa` Particle formal archive kept
the measured RTDL native DSO and public PyOptiX PTX, but not RTDL's generated
leaf source/PTX, wrapper source/PTX, composed PTX, or any timed worker's full
traversal receipt. The formal workers retained compact physical evidence plus
program/executable identities. The original report's phrase "all
executable/evidence bytes" was inaccurate.

No original formal artifact was rewritten. The supplement
`POSTFORMAL_RECONSTRUCTION.tar.gz` is a new, explicitly post-formal
same-source/toolchain reconstruction and one untimed correctness replay.
